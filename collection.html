Why I made it

Whenever someone recommended a book or a film, I wrote it down in a notes app. But the list kept growing and it became hard to find anything. So I decided to build myself an easy app, one I would actually enjoy opening.

Why I'm sharing it

Talking about it with friends, I realised they had the same need. I started letting them try it, and today they use it every day. So I wanted to open it to everyone.

What it does

Who recommended it

Write down who recommended a title, so you never forget.

Four types in one place

Books, films, shows and video games, sorted by where you're at.

Entries that fill themselves

Type a title or scan a book: the entry fills itself in.

Ideas by Claude

Suggestions picked for your taste, with the reason behind each one.

Where to watch

For every film or show, where you can watch it.

Your year in numbers

What you read, watched and played this year, and your favourites.

Made to share

A link to show your wishlist or favourites to friends and family, no account needed.

Everywhere, even offline

Installs on your phone, works without a connection, in English and French.

Want to try it?

It's free. You can also take a look at the demo, no account needed.
