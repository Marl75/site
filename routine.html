Why I made it

I wanted a simple, effective app to log the exercises from my workouts and track my progress. None of the existing apps really suited me, to the point that I cancelled the one I was paying for: I decided to build my own. I now use it every day, and since my friends liked it too, I wanted to share it.

How it grew

I had people around me test it, and every piece of feedback helped the app evolve: an easier start, faster sets to log, a clearer progress view.

What it does

Your usual workouts, ready made

Save a usual workout, like “Upper body”: next time, all its exercises are ready, with your numbers.

Pick up where you left off

Your weights and reps from last time are right there: you know where you stood, and can aim a little higher. Logging a set takes a second.

Exercises suited to your goals

Pick the muscles you want to strengthen: the app suggests suitable exercises.

Your week at a glance

See right away whether you hit your workout goal for the week. A nice nudge to go back.

Your progress

A curve for each exercise, and a nudge when it's time to add weight.

Tips to do each exercise right

For each exercise, the right moves to avoid getting hurt.

Everywhere, even offline

Installs on your phone, works without a connection at the gym, in English and French.

Want to try it?

It is free. The demo opens on a sample account with twelve weeks of workouts: nothing to create.
