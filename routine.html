Why I made it

To track my workouts, I used a paid app that never quite did what I wanted. I wanted an app that remembers everything from last time, weights, reps and machine setup, so I can log a set in one tap between efforts, and that tells me whether I'm really working the muscles I'm aiming for.

How it grew

I had people around me test it, and every piece of feedback changed something: an easier start, simpler sets, a clearer progress view. It is now ready for everyone.

What it does

Everything is already filled in

Weights, reps, machine setup, speed and incline: each workout picks up what you did last time.

Your usual workouts in one tap

Save a workout as a routine, then start it again in one tap next time.

One set, one tap

A table of sets with a check for each; weight and reps are set with two matching counters.

Your target muscles

Pick the muscles to develop or go easy on: the right exercises come first, the others are flagged.

Your week at a glance

Days each muscle was worked and your cardio minutes, against the ACSM and WHO benchmarks.

Your progress

A curve for every exercise, and a weight suggestion when you reach your rep target.

Do every exercise right

A figure showing the movement and the muscles worked, with tips from reliable sources.

Everywhere, even offline

Installs on your phone, works without a connection at the gym, in English and French.

Want to try it?

It is free. The demo opens on a sample account with twelve weeks of workouts: nothing to create.
